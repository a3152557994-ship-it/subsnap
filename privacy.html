SubSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

SubSnap ("the App", "we") is developed to respect your privacy in the most literal way possible: we cannot see your data, because it never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The App:

  • Does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data
  • Does not contain analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind
  • Does not require an account, sign-up, or login
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, or microphone

All subscriptions you add are stored locally on your device only. You can export them to a file at any time from Settings → Export, and import them back on any device. If you delete the App, the data is deleted with it.

Notifications

If you enable renewal reminders, the App schedules notifications on your device using Apple's standard notification system. These notifications are generated locally and are never sent through or stored on any server. You can disable them anytime in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time "SubSnap Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your identity. Purchase restoration is handled by Apple with your Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy applies to App Store transactions.

Children

The App contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Legal rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we collect no personal data, there is no data for us to access, correct, delete, or transfer. Your subscription data is under your sole control on your device, and can be exported or erased by you directly.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date, and material changes will be reflected in the App's release notes.
